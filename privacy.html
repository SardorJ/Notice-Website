Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Notice

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Notice – Mindful Screen Time is developed and owned by Sardor Jurayev, who is responsible for this policy. On the App Store it is distributed through Djamshid Jurayev's developer account. It is also on Google Play. This policy explains what the iPhone and Android apps store and how this website counts page views.

No account, nothing leaves your iPhone, Notice only knows the apps you connect. The app has no analytics and no ads. The App Store privacy label for Notice is Data Not Collected. On Android, nothing leaves your phone. There is no account and there are no ads.

What We Collect

There is no account. Notice does not require a login or any personal information to function.

On iPhone

  • Notice stores, only on your iPhone, the names of the apps you connect, a session log kept for 120 days, and a Shortcuts run log. Nothing is sent anywhere. That data is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Timers, notifications, and alerts (via Apple's AlarmKit) run locally on your device.
  • Widgets and Dynamic Island displays run locally on your device.
  • The iPhone app does not use analytics or advertising, and it does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

On Android

Notice is on Google Play. Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account and there are no ads. What the app keeps stays on the phone.

  • Usage access. Notice reads usage events on the phone to know when a connected app is in front. You choose the apps. Nothing is preselected.
  • Display over other apps. This lets the floating timer appear over a connected app while that app is in front.
  • Foreground notification. A silent “Notice is on” notification stays while Notice is watching. Android requires it for a foreground service.
  • Notice keeps the apps you connected, and your sessions and stats, on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Crash Reports

If the iOS app crashes, Apple's built-in diagnostic tools may collect anonymous crash logs to help us fix bugs. This is handled entirely by Apple's operating system-level reporting, not by Notice directly. You can review or turn this off in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

This Website

This website uses GoatCounter, cookie-free page-view analytics, at https://noticeapp.goatcounter.com. GoatCounter keeps aggregate page-view statistics. It uses no cookies and does no cross-site tracking. GoatCounter's privacy policy is at https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Notice is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children. What the app stores stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Changes to This Policy

If Notice introduces new features that involve data collection (for example, optional future account-based features), this policy will be updated and the in-app privacy details — including Apple's App Privacy information and Google Play's Data safety form — will be revised accordingly.

Contact

Sardor Jurayev is responsible for Notice and for this policy. If you have questions, contact Noticemindfullscreentime@gmail.com.
