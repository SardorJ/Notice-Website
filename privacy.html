Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Notice

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Notice – Mindful Screen Time is developed and owned by Sardor Jurayev, who is responsible for this policy. It is distributed on the App Store through Djamshid Jurayev's developer account. This policy explains what the iPhone app stores and how this website counts page views.

No account, nothing leaves your iPhone, Notice only knows the apps you connect. The app has no analytics and no ads. The App Store privacy label for Notice is Data Not Collected.

What We Collect

Notice itself does not send data to us or to anyone else. There is no account. What the app keeps stays on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it.

  • Notice does not require an account, login, or any personal information to function.
  • The app does not use analytics or advertising, and it does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

On iPhone

  • Notice stores, only on your iPhone, the names of the apps you connect, a session log kept for 120 days, and a Shortcuts run log. Nothing is sent anywhere. That data is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Timers, notifications, and alerts (via Apple's AlarmKit) run locally on your device.
  • Widgets and Dynamic Island displays run locally on your device.

On Android

Not yet. It's coming soon to Google Play, for Android 8.0 and later. This policy covers the iPhone app and this website. It will be updated before an Android version is published.

Crash Reports

If the iOS app crashes, Apple's built-in diagnostic tools may collect anonymous crash logs to help us fix bugs. This is handled entirely by Apple's operating system-level reporting, not by Notice directly. You can review or turn this off in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

This Website

This website uses GoatCounter, cookie-free page-view analytics, at https://noticeapp.goatcounter.com. GoatCounter keeps aggregate page-view statistics. It uses no cookies and does no cross-site tracking. GoatCounter's privacy policy is at https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Notice is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children. What the app stores stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Changes to This Policy

If Notice introduces new features that involve data collection (for example, optional future account-based features, or a published Android version), this policy will be updated and the in-app privacy details — including Apple's App Privacy information and, once an Android version is published, Google Play's Data safety form — will be revised accordingly.

Contact

Sardor Jurayev is responsible for Notice and for this policy. If you have questions, contact Noticemindfullscreentime@gmail.com.
